# Starter topics: mobile app

Add to `common.md`.

## Stores
- **Store accounts** (Apple, Google): owner, legal entity, who can publish. Moving an app between accounts later is painful.
- **Store review rules**: payments, account deletion in the app, user-generated content, kids category.
- **In-app purchases versus outside payment**: store rules decide which is allowed, and the fees.
- **Age rating questionnaire** and what it implies for content and ads.
- **App name, bundle identifier, signing keys**: the identifier and the Android signing key are hard or impossible to change once published.

## Users and accounts
- **Sign-in methods**: guest, email, Apple and Google sign-in (Apple requires its own when others are offered).
- **Account deletion from inside the app**: required by the stores.
- **Data on device versus server**, and what happens on a new phone.

## Quality
- **Oldest OS versions and devices supported**: drives testing cost.
- **Offline behaviour and poor networks**.
- **Battery, data use, app size**.
- **Crash reporting** and its privacy impact.
- **Accessibility**: text size, screen readers, colour contrast.

## Release
- **Beta channel** (TestFlight, closed testing) and who is in it.
- **Forced updates**: what happens when an old version can no longer talk to the server.
- **Localisation**: languages, prices in local currency.
- **Push notifications**: consent, frequency, content.
