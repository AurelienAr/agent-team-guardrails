# Starter topics: online game

Add to `common.md`, and to `mobile-app.md` for a mobile game.

## Game design
- **Core loop**: what the player does every session, and why they come back tomorrow.
- **First minutes**: tutorial, first goal, when the player first feels clever.
- **Progression and its pace**: how long to the end of the content, what happens after.
- **Balance**: who owns the numbers, how they are tested, how they change after launch.
- **Content plan**: seasons, events, what is ready at launch versus later.

## Players together
- **Matchmaking or server choice**, new servers, merging servers.
- **Guilds, alliances, friends**: limits, roles, what happens when a leader leaves.
- **Chat**: channels, filtering, reporting, moderation, who moderates.
- **Cheating and multi-accounting**: what is allowed, what is banned, how it is detected.
- **Bots and automation**.

## Money
- **Business model**: premium, free-to-play, subscription, ads.
- **What can be bought**, and whether it gives power ("pay to win").
- **Randomised paid rewards (loot boxes)**: displayed odds, countries where they are restricted (for example Belgium), minors.
- **Virtual currencies**: refunds, what happens to unspent currency at shutdown.
- **Prices**: local currencies, store fees, no fake urgency.

## Players' safety and law
- **Age gate and minors**: spending limits, chat restrictions.
- **Personal data of players** and of chat messages.
- **Ratings** (PEGI, ESRB, store ratings).
- **Shutdown policy**: notice period, refunds, what players keep.

## Running it
- **Server authority**: what the client may decide, what only the server decides.
- **Outages during a fight or event**: who wins, what is refunded.
- **Live operations**: patches, maintenance windows, communication with players.
- **Community**: where players talk to the team (Discord, forum), who answers.
- **Assets**: origin and licence of every image, sound and model, including AI-generated ones.
