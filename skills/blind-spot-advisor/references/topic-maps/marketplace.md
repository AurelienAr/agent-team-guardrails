# Starter topics: marketplace

Add to `common.md`.

## Two sides
- **Which side comes first**, and how the empty marketplace is filled at launch.
- **Seller or provider onboarding**: identity checks, approval, quality bar.
- **Listings**: rules, forbidden items or services, who reviews them.
- **Search and ranking**: what decides who is shown first, and whether it can be bought.

## Money
- **Fees**: who pays, how much, when.
- **Payments**: who holds the money, when sellers are paid, payment provider rules for marketplaces.
- **Taxes and invoices**: who issues them, platform reporting duties for sellers (for example DAC7 in the EU).
- **Refunds, chargebacks and fraud**.

## Trust
- **Disputes**: how a buyer and a seller who disagree are separated, who decides, in how long.
- **Reviews and ratings**: who can leave one, fake reviews, removal rules.
- **Messaging between users**, and keeping deals on the platform.
- **Safety** for in-person services.

## Law
- **Platform liability** for what users list or do.
- **Consumer law**: when sellers are professionals versus individuals.
- **Notice and takedown** of illegal content (for example the Digital Services Act in the EU).
