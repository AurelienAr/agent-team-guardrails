# Starter topics: any project

Topics almost every project must settle, whatever it builds. Start the map from this list, then add the list for the project's kind. Each line says why the topic bites when it is skipped.

## Ownership and decisions
- **Who decides what**: without it, the assistant fills the gaps and nobody notices.
- **How a decision is recorded**: a decision that lives only in a chat is lost at the next session.
- **What counts as approval**: see the signature-is-not-proof skill.
- **What happens when two decision-makers disagree**.

## Money
- **Budget and recurring costs** (hosting, services, licences): small monthly costs add up unseen.
- **Who pays, and on which account**: personal cards on company services cause trouble later.
- **Pricing and refunds**, if anything is sold.

## Legal
- **Who owns the code and the assets** (company, founders, contractors).
- **Licences of dependencies and assets**: one incompatible licence can block a release.
- **Terms of use and privacy policy**, if users exist.
- **Personal data**: what is collected, where it is stored, how long, how it is deleted (GDPR in Europe).
- **Age of users**, if minors can use it.

## Security
- **Where secrets live** and who can read them.
- **Accounts and access**: who has admin, how access is removed when someone leaves.
- **What an AI agent may do on its own**, and what needs a human.

## Operations
- **Backups and restore**, tested at least once: an untested backup is a hope.
- **Monitoring and alerts**: who learns first that it is down.
- **Incident response**: who does what when it breaks, and what users are told.
- **Environments**: test, staging, production, and how a change moves between them.

## Product
- **Who the users are**, and the one problem the first version solves.
- **What "done" means for the first version**.
- **Support**: how a user reports a problem, and who answers.
- **End of life**: what happens to users and data if the project stops.
