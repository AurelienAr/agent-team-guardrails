# Starter topics: open source library or tool

Add to `common.md` (the money and user-data parts may not apply).

## Licence and ownership
- **Licence choice**, and whether it fits the licences of dependencies.
- **Contributor terms**: none, DCO sign-off, or a contributor licence agreement.
- **Trademark** of the project name, if it matters.

## Releases
- **Versioning scheme** (semantic versioning or not) and what counts as a breaking change.
- **Supported versions** of the language, runtime and platforms.
- **Release process**: who can publish, signing, changelog.
- **Package registry accounts** and two-factor authentication on them.

## Community
- **Contribution guide**: how to propose a change, review expectations.
- **Code of conduct** and who enforces it.
- **Issue triage**: labels, who answers, what is out of scope.
- **Governance**: who has the final word, how maintainers are added or removed.

## Security
- **Security policy**: how to report a vulnerability privately, response time.
- **Dependency updates** and supply-chain checks.
- **Secrets in CI** and who can trigger workflows from forks.

## Sustainability
- **Funding**: sponsors, none, or a company behind it.
- **Bus factor**: what happens if the main maintainer stops.
