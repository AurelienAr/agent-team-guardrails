# Starter topics: SaaS / web application

Add to `common.md`.

## Customers and billing
- **Plans, limits and what happens at the limit**.
- **Trials**: length, card required or not, what happens at the end.
- **Billing**: provider, invoices, VAT and sales tax by country, failed payments, dunning.
- **Refunds and cancellation**: self-serve or not, pro-rata or not.
- **Companies as customers**: purchase orders, invoices with company details, contracts.

## Accounts and access
- **Teams and roles** inside a customer account.
- **Single sign-on** for larger customers.
- **Account deletion and data export** on request.

## Data
- **Multi-tenancy**: how one customer's data is kept from another's.
- **Where data is hosted** (region), asked by European and enterprise customers.
- **Data processing agreement** and sub-processors list.
- **Retention**: logs, backups, deleted accounts.

## Reliability
- **Uptime target** and whether it is promised (SLA) or not.
- **Status page** and how incidents are announced.
- **Rate limits and abuse**: what stops one customer from slowing everyone down.
- **Database migrations without downtime**.

## Growth
- **Onboarding**: the first ten minutes of a new user.
- **Analytics** and its consent banner.
- **Transactional emails**: sender domain, deliverability, unsubscribe.
- **Public API**: versioning, keys, deprecation.
